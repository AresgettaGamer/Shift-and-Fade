# Shift & Fade: Waystones — v1.0.0

First stable public release, promoted from the runtime-approved Beta v0.9.8 line.

- Persistent private/public Waystone network with names, discovery and favorites.
- Distance-based XP travel costs and cross-dimensional travel.
- Warp Stone, Return Scroll, Death Scroll and Home Scroll.
- Linked Teleport Pads with free travel and anti-bounce handling.
- Natural Overworld Waystones and biome-aware village sanctuaries.
- PBR/Vibrant Visuals-ready presentation and custom sounds.
- Shift & Fade Protocol v2 integration with standalone player-only fallback.
- Optional WATI Core/Codex and WATI Lens Providers, including dynamic Lens data.
- Mexican Spanish and US English localization.
- Stable promotion only: no redesign of gameplay approved in Beta v0.9.8.
